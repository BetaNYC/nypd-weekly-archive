Quality of life: what we saved, precinct by precinct

NYPD's quality-of-life report (its Q-Team files) counts 911 and 311 calls for service in each precinct: noise, vehicle-related calls such as illegal parking, blocked driveways and double parking, and disorderly behavior. It runs a few weeks behind NYPD's other weekly files, and each week's file replaces the last.

This archive began saving it in November 2026.
