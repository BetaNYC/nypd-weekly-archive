NYCHA Housing Bureau report: what we saved, week by week

Every week NYPD's Housing Bureau posts a report on the seven major crimes in each public-housing development, by Police Service Area and precinct. The next week's file replaces it.

This archive began saving the spreadsheet and the PDF in November 2026. Their numbers are not read into CSV yet, so for now the files themselves are the record. Each square below is one week.
